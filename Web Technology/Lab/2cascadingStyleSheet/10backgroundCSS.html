House Party - VVUP

VVUP (Korean: 비비업; RR: bibieop) is a South Korean girl group formed by EgoENT in 2024. The group consists of four members: Kim, Paan, Suyeon, and Jiyoon. Former member Hyunny departed from the group in August 2024 due to health issues.
